Privacy Policy

Moment · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Moment is a personal life-journal app for iPhone. This policy describes what the app stores on your device and what limited information may leave your device when you use the app. It reflects how Moment works today.

No accounts

Moment does not require an account, email address, or sign-in. There is no Moment cloud login and we do not operate a user profile system for your journal.

What stays on your device

Your journal content is stored locally on your iPhone, including:

  • Notes and captions you write
  • Photos you capture or add from your library (as files in the app’s storage)
  • Optional location details attached to a moment (coordinates and/or a place name)
  • App preferences (for example appearance, reminder settings, gallery-save toggle)
  • Data used by the Moment Home Screen widget via an App Group on the same device

Moments are kept with SwiftData and local files. They are not uploaded to a Moment server. Deleting the app removes Moment’s local store unless you have separately saved photos into Apple Photos (see below).

Permissions you may grant

  • Camera — to capture new photos as moments
  • Photo Library — to browse and add existing photos, and optionally save captures into Photos
  • Location (when in use) — to tag new camera captures with where they were taken
  • Notifications — optional daily reminder to capture a moment
  • Local Network — declared for optional nearby collaboration (see below)

Services that may leave your device

Analytics (PostHog)

Moment uses PostHog to understand how the app is used (for example app opens, screen views, and interface interactions). Analytics traffic is sent to PostHog (currently configured for the US PostHog ingestion host). Session replay / screen recording is turned off in the app.

Analytics may include device and usage information such as an anonymous app instance identifier, device type / OS version, and interaction events. Moment does not send your photo files or journal notes as analytics content. PostHog’s own processing is described in their documentation and privacy materials.

Apple system services

  • Reverse geocoding — when a moment has coordinates, Moment may ask Apple’s geocoder for a human-readable place name. That request uses the coordinates you already have on-device.
  • Apple Photos — if you leave “Phone Gallery Saves” on (or otherwise save to Photos), camera captures can be written into your Photo Library on this device.
  • Share Sheet — if you share a day collage or the app, the content you choose is handed to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, etc.). Moment does not operate those destinations.
  • Local notifications — daily reminders are scheduled on-device via Apple’s notification system.

Nearby day sharing (peer-to-peer)

The app includes Multipeer Connectivity code so nearby devices can optionally exchange moments for a shared day over Bluetooth / peer-to-peer Wi‑Fi, without a Moment cloud account. When that sharing session is active, moment content (including photos and notes for that day) can be sent directly to connected nearby devices. Moment does not route that content through our servers.

Tracking

Moment does not use App Tracking Transparency / IDFA advertising tracking, and does not use analytics for cross-app advertising networks. Analytics are for understanding and improving Moment.

Children

Moment is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through a Moment account (Moment has no accounts).

Changes

If how Moment handles data changes in a material way, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above.
