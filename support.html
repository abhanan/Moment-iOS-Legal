FAQ

  • Where are my moments stored?
    On your iPhone. Moment keeps your journal locally and does not require a Moment account.
  • Do I need an account?
    No. Open the app and start capturing.
  • Why does Moment ask for Photos access?
    So you can add photos from your library into a day, and optionally save new camera captures into Apple Photos.
  • Can I get a reminder to capture?
    Yes. After your first moment, Moment may offer a daily reminder. You can manage reminders in Settings and in iOS Settings → Notifications.
  • What is Memory Lane?
    A drive-style way to revisit moments you’ve already saved — each memory shows at most once per drive.
  • How do I share a day?
    Use Share to open the day canvas, arrange stamps, then export via the system share sheet.
  • I deleted the app — are my moments gone?
    Moment’s private store is removed with the app. Photos you separately saved into Apple Photos remain in Photos.
